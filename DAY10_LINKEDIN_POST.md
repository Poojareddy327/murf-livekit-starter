🎉 **Graduated! Completed 10 Days of Voice Agents — VoiceForBharat Edition!** 🇮🇳🎙️

Over the last 10 days, I built **FinAssist**, an intelligent, multilingual Financial Services Voice AI Assistant designed to make banking guidance and government scheme discovery accessible to every Indian citizen.

### 🌟 What I Built & Learned:
- **Streaming Indian TTS**: Powered by **Murf Falcon** for ultra-low latency, warm, and natural conversational responses.
- **Multilingual & Code-Mixed Support**: Understood and responded in native Indian scripts (Devanagari) and Hindi-English code-mixed dialogue.
- **Consent-First Memory & Guardrails**: Persistent caller memory, explicit consent checks, right-to-be-forgotten privacy, and strict PII sanitization.
- **Government Scheme Calculator**: Instant eligibility checks for PMAY, PM Jan Dhan, Sukanya Samriddhi, and Mudra loans based on age, income, and employment.
- **Human Escalation Protocol**: Automated emergency support ticketing with duplicate resolution and webhook notifications.
- **Specialist Handoff & Multi-Voice Engine**: Seamless handoffs to dedicated scheme specialists and context-based voice switching.
- **Live Call Analytics Dashboard**: Real-time monitoring of call outcome logs and performance metrics.

Building this agent taught me how to combine real-time WebRTC streaming transport with intelligent LLM function calling while prioritizing user privacy and low latency.

A massive thank you to **@Murf AI** for hosting this hands-on, high-impact challenge! 🚀
